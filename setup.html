PC Remote setup

Three quick steps on your phone. This page only opens on this PC and updates by itself.

Put Tailscale on your phone

Install the free Tailscale app and sign in with the same account you used on this PC. Leave it turned on.

Scan this with your phone camera to get the app, or search "Tailscale" in the App Store / Google Play.
